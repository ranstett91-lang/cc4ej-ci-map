⚑ Claymont Focus (ZIP 19703)

Claymont is sandwiched between I-95, I-495, the Delaware River, and a dense industrial corridor — including a steel mill, sulfuric acid plant, specialty chemical plant, Honeywell Superfund site, and 343 acres of contaminated former steel land under active DNREC cleanup. Cross-state pollution from Marcus Hook, PA is also a named concern.

"Claymont is between two highways." — Resident, Sept. 2025 listening session (47 attendees)

About the scores

Pollution Burden — How much air pollution, toxic waste, and chemical risk a neighborhood faces. Higher = more burden.

Vulnerability — How many people in the neighborhood may have a harder time staying healthy (low income, unemployment, language barriers). Higher = more vulnerable.

DelDOT Equity Areas — Communities that Delaware's transportation agency identified as needing extra attention.

⚠ Name ⚠ = Data gap neighborhood. Census block group boundaries include schools, commercial areas, or other non-residential land that dilutes the real burden on residents. Scores shown are likely lower than the true impact.
Data sources: EPA EJScreen 2023, EPA TRI, CDC PLACES 2022, DelDOT EFA 2024, DNREC Air Permits & VCP Sites, CC4EJ Community Listening Sessions.
